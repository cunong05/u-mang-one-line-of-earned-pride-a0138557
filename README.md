Entry: **"After three failed harvests, the old farmer held up one perfect tomato and just stood there smiling at it."** (106 characters)

## Why this line

Three failed harvests are the effort the reader can feel — years of work lost, not a trophy handed over. The pride is earned inside the line: one perfect tomato, held up, and a man who just stands there smiling at it. No podium, no graduation, no congratulations. A concrete scene, a real person, and the pride arrives by the last word.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly the entry — 106 bytes, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to 1–240 characters on a single line, and verifies the file's sha256 after writing.
